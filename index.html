01 — INTRODUCTION

My Beautiful Country

Discover the beauty of Uzbekistan.

Welcome to Uzbekistan!

Uzbekistan is a country in Central Asia. It is famous for its ancient cities, beautiful buildings and interesting history.

The capital city is Tashkent. Uzbekistan shares borders with Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Afghanistan and Turkmenistan.

Uzbekistan is a country of friendly people, beautiful traditions and delicious national food. I am proud of my country!
